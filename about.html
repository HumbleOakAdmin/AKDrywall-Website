01 / ABOUT THE COMPANY

20 YEARS IN BUSINESS.
A STRONG FOUNDATION.

For 20 years, A&K Drywall has been part of Edmonton’s construction industry. Founded in 2006 by Joga Dhothar and his brothers, A&K Drywall is a family-owned and operated company built on hard work, dependable relationships and pride in our craft. For the past 10 years, Joga’s family has continued to lead the business, carrying forward the values it was founded on.

Our 20-year history began in residential construction. Over the past decade, we have expanded our focus to commercial and multifamily projects. Today, we bring that experience to projects of every scale, from individual homes to large developments.

Our team provides a comprehensive range of drywall and interior finishing services in-house, giving builders one point of contact for coordinated work from early preparation through final finishes.
